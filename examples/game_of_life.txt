Implement Conway's Game of Life in the terminal. Start with a random
grid of cells (alive or dead). Accept width, height, and number of
generations as command-line arguments (defaults: 60x30, 100 generations).

Display each generation by clearing the screen and redrawing. Use █ for
alive cells and space for dead cells. Show the generation number and
population count at the top. Add a small delay between frames (50ms)
to create an animation effect.

Rules:
- Any live cell with 2 or 3 neighbors survives
- Any dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive
- All other cells die or stay dead
